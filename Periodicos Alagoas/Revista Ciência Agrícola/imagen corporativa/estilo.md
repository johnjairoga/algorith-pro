# Revista Ciência Agrícola — Guía de Estilo

## Identidad Visual

Key differences from Crítica Histórica: This system uses a modern dark palette with bright gold accents, organic curved dividers instead of rounded rectangles, and contemporary scientific imagery instead of vintage historical photos. The aesthetic is forward-looking and tech-focused rather than academic/archival.

---

## Three Colours. Fixed Roles.

**Rule: 3 colours, fixed roles, every post.**

| Role | Colour | Hex | Usage |
|------|--------|-----|-------|
| **PRIMARY** | Bright Gold / Yellow | `#F4C430` | Defines identity and emphasis. Accent words, key highlights, logo accents, focal points. |
| **BACKGROUND** | Dark Forest Green | `#1B4D3E` | Neutral canvas. Primary background for text areas. Maintains modern, scientific aesthetic. |
| **ACCENT** | White | `#FFFFFF` | Emphasis and structure. Headlines, main text, organic dividers, high-contrast elements. |

**Application:**
- Every post uses all 3 colours in these fixed roles
- Primary gold (#F4C430) drives visual hierarchy through accent words
- Dark green background (#1B4D3E) ensures professional authority
- White (#FFFFFF) provides high contrast and legibility
- Organic curved divider separates visual and text columns
